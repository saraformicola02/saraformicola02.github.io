Hydrological and Hydraulic Risk Assessment of the Mignone River Basin, Including Hydraulic Structure Design

This project develops an integrated hydraulic flood risk assessment for the Mignone River Basin in the Lazio Region, Italy, with the aim of identifying flood-prone areas and designing structural mitigation measures. The study combines hydrological, hydraulic, geospatial, and economic analyses to evaluate flood hazard, exposure, and potential damage within the watershed, with particular attention to a proposed industrial development located in a flood-prone area. The initial phase involved the characterization of the basin through territorial, land-use, geomorphological, and climatic analyses. Historical meteorological data were processed to investigate precipitation, temperature, and discharge patterns, while GIS-based tools were used to examine the watershed and its drainage network. Rainfall-runoff modelling was then performed using HEC-HMS, followed by hydraulic simulations in HEC-RAS to assess floodplain extent and water levels under different return-period scenarios. Flood hazard, damage, and risk maps were subsequently developed using QGIS by combining hydraulic outputs with land-use and exposure information. Based on the results, a structural flood mitigation intervention was proposed: the design of an approximately 2.1 km earth levee system intended to reduce flood risk and protect the planned industrial facility. The levee geometry was assessed through hydraulic and seepage considerations, including the design of a land-side berm to improve safety against seepage-related failure. The proposed intervention was implemented in HEC-RAS to evaluate its effectiveness under the design flood scenario. A cost-benefit analysis was also conducted to compare expected flood damages before and after the intervention with the estimated construction costs. The results indicate a substantial reduction in inundated areas and expected economic losses, demonstrating the potential effectiveness and economic feasibility of the proposed mitigation measure. The project provided practical experience in hydrological and hydraulic modelling, flood risk mapping, engineering design, geospatial analysis, and economic evaluation, highlighting the importance of an integrated approach to climate adaptation, territorial planning, and flood risk management.
