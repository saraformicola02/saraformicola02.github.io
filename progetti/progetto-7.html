Sustainable Territorial Development and Strategic Planning for the La Maddalena Archipelago: A Pilot Project for Regenerating the Former Military Arsenal

This project investigates the sustainable development and strategic planning of the La Maddalena Archipelago, combining territorial, environmental, social, and economic analyses to understand the complex relationship between local communities, tourism, and the protected natural environment. The study examines the main challenges affecting the area, including seasonal tourism pressure, the vulnerability of coastal and marine ecosystems, and the need to strengthen the local economy beyond the summer season. Particular attention is given to the regeneration of the former Military Arsenal, a strategically located site near the harbour and the main tourist routes. The proposed intervention introduces a pilot Visitor Centre conceived as a multifunctional hub for visitor information, sustainable mobility, environmental awareness, and territorial valorisation. The project aims to improve visitor orientation, encourage responsible access to the archipelago, and connect tourism activities with local businesses and environmental initiatives. Ultimately, the proposal seeks to support a more balanced and resilient development model, strengthening La Maddalena’s identity as a National Park and promoting a sustainable economy throughout the year.
